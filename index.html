Our Story

WE'RE MORE
THAN WINGS.

We're a feeling. A vibe. A place where good food meets great people, and every meal becomes a memory.

The Beginning

BORN FROM
A PASSION
FOR FLAVOR

Boneless & Chill started as a simple dream: create the perfect boneless wing experience in an atmosphere that felt like home — but cooler. Our founders spent years perfecting recipes, sourcing quality ingredients, and designing a space where flavor meets comfort.

Today, we're proud to be more than a restaurant. We're a community gathering spot, a celebration venue, a weekly tradition, and a place where every guest feels like family.

What Drives Us

OUR THREE PILLARS

Everything we do comes back to these three core values that shape every wing, every plate, every experience.

PASSION FOR FOOD

Our kitchen team puts heart into every single plate. We obsess over flavors, textures, and the perfect sauce ratio so you don't have to.

QUALITY INGREDIENTS

We source fresh, premium chicken and make our sauces in-house daily. No shortcuts, no compromises. Just real, honest ingredients.

FRIENDLY ATMOSPHERE

The food is the reason you come. The vibes are the reason you stay. Our space is designed to feel like the coolest living room you've ever been in.

BOLD FLAVORS

From mild and sweet to face-meltingly hot, we celebrate flavor in all its forms. There's something here for every palate and every mood.

COME SEE FOR
YOURSELF.

Words only go so far. The best way to understand B&C is to be in the room.
